BNPL Tracker

A buy now, pay later tracker for iPhone and iPad.

Connect your email and see every pay-later plan you have, what is still to pay this month and when each payment is due. Your email is read on your iPhone and never leaves it.

What it does

  • Finds plans from the emails Klarna, Afterpay, Affirm, Zip, Sezzle and PayPal send you
  • Shows each plan's payments, their dates and what is left to pay
  • Puts every payment on a calendar, and on your iPhone's calendar if you turn that on
  • Reminds you the day before a payment
  • Shows the email each plan came from
  • Opens the provider's own page when you want to pay; the app never moves money
