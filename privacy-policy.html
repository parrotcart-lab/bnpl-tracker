Privacy Policy

BNPL Tracker, the buy now, pay later tracker.

Last updated 2 October 2026

Your email never leaves your phone. BNPL Tracker reads your pay-later emails on your iPhone or iPad to find your plans. Nothing it reads is sent to us or to anyone else, and we run no server that could receive it.

What the app reads

Email. You connect Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo, AOL or another provider with an app password you make in that account's security settings. The app signs in to your provider's mail server directly from your device, opens your mailbox read-only, and searches only for emails from or naming Klarna, Afterpay, Affirm, Zip, Sezzle and PayPal's pay-later plans. From those it reads the store, the amounts, the payment dates and whether a payment was received or refunded. Anything else it fetches is dropped on your device.

The app cannot send, delete, move or change your email, and reading an email does not mark it as read.

Purchases you add by hand. You can type a plan in yourself. It is kept the same way as one found in email.

What is stored, and where

  • The plans and payments the app finds, with the subject line and the opening lines of each email so you can see where a figure came from. These stay in the app's own storage on your device, and in your device's own backups if you have those on.
  • Each app password, in the iOS Keychain. It is synced by iCloud Keychain if you use it, so a new iPhone doesn't need setting up again. Apple encrypts it end to end; we can't see it.
  • Your settings and the reminders the app has scheduled, on your device.
  • If you turn on the calendar, a calendar named for the app with one event per payment (the store, the provider and the amount). It is made in your default calendar account, so if that account is iCloud or another service, those events sync through it like your other calendar events. Turning the calendar off deletes it and its events.

What leaves your device

Nothing the app reads is sent to us. The app connects straight from your device to your email provider to search and read those emails, and that connection never passes through us. Calendar events sync only through your own calendar account, as above. Links you open, such as a provider's payment page, open that website.

Usage information, advertising and tracking

There is none. The app has no analytics, no advertising identifier, no ads and no tracking across apps or websites. We have no account system and no way to know who uses the app.

Deleting your data

Remove an email account in the app and its app password is deleted and the app stops reading that mailbox. Plans already found stay until you delete them: open a plan, tap the menu and choose Delete purchase. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on that device. To stop access at the source, delete the app password in your email account's security settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
