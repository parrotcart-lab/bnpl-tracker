BNPL Tracker, the buy now, pay later tracker.

Email parrotcart@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing and what happened. Please don't forward your pay-later emails to us unless we ask — we never need to see your mailbox.

Connecting your email

Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo and AOL each need an app password instead of your normal one. The app walks you through making it in your account's security settings, then you paste it in. Gmail offers app passwords once 2-Step Verification is on.

Outlook and Hotmail accounts can't be connected yet, because Microsoft requires its own sign-in. You can still add your purchases by hand.

A plan is missing

The app finds plans from the emails the pay-later companies send. If the confirmation went to an address you haven't connected, or was deleted, the plan won't show. Add it by hand with Add, or connect that other address.

A payment shows as "possibly past due"

The app sees your email, not the provider's records. If a payment's day has passed and no "payment received" email has arrived, the app says it is possibly past due. If you have paid, open the plan and choose Mark as paid. Check the provider's own app for the true status.

An amount or date looks wrong

Open the plan and tap Found in to see the email it was read from. If the email says something different from what the app shows, please write to us with the provider's name and what the email says (not the email itself).

Paying

The app never moves money. Pay in Klarna (or your provider) opens that company's own page or app, and the payment happens there.

Calendar and reminders

Turn on Reminders to get a notification the day before each payment, and Add to iPhone Calendar to see payments beside your other events. Turning the calendar off removes its events.

Removing an email account or a plan

In Alerts, find the account and choose Remove. Its app password is deleted and the app stops reading that mailbox; plans already found stay. To delete a plan, open it, tap the menu and choose Delete purchase. To stop access at the source too, delete the app password in that account's security settings.
